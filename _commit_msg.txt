Fix critical packaging bug: 6 modules missing from PyInstaller spec, 3 method-prefix mappings missing

The packaged avs-backend.exe was silently missing 6 feature modules that are
loaded dynamically via importlib.import_module() in rpc_server.py but were not
listed in the PyInstaller spec's hiddenimports. This caused these features to
fail with "Unknown method" in the packaged app while working fine in dev:

  - avs_backend.network_optimizer (Internet Booster, TCP/IP tuning)
  - avs_backend.junk_monitor (junk accumulation monitoring)
  - avs_backend.disk_optimizer (defrag, SSD TRIM)
  - avs_backend.pup_scanner (PUP detection)
  - avs_backend.browser_extensions (extension manager)
  - avs_backend.context_menu (context menu manager)

Additionally, 3 modules had mismatched RPC method prefixes that prevented the
dispatch logic from waiting for them to load:

  - network_opt.* -> avs_backend.network_optimizer (was: network_optimizer)
  - pup.* -> avs_backend.pup_scanner (was: pup_scanner)
  - browser_ext.* -> avs_backend.browser_extensions (was: browser_extensions)

Added all 6 modules to avs-backend.spec hiddenimports and added explicit
prefix mappings in rpc_server.py _explicit dict.

Verified: all 12 critical RPCs now respond correctly in the packaged binary.
